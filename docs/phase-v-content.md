## Evidence rules

- Confirmed business facts come from the shared business configuration and the signed project brief. Manufacturer manuals describe product features and model-dependent recommendations without establishing service compatibility. A vehicle match depends on the exact vehicle, market, existing key type, parts and tools. No model-year coverage matrix is published.
- Preserve existing publication dates and bylines. If the facts, advice or wording materially change, record the actual revision date. Use the business as publisher. Named authors, technical review, precise model-year coverage and on-site capability need direct verification. Drafts without evidence stay out of the published registry.
- An editor checks scope, originality, sources, customer safety, photo provenance, accessibility, bilingual meaning, metadata and internal links before release. Correct errors at their existing canonical and record the correction where material. Review automotive manual links when manufacturers update manuals and pricing language when the company updates policies.
- Genuine portfolio submissions require clearly licensed originals, a publication approval record and a redacted job summary confirming general area, category, problem, work and outcome. A verified job record can supply a guide-compatible headline and summary, supported photos, service link and confirmed outcome. Publishable assets exclude plates, VINs, addresses, customer details, key codes and access configurations. No empty or fictional portfolio route is registered.
- For high-priority compatibility evidence, request actual supported key families, markets and model-year ranges, whether an existing working key is needed, immobilizer/module exceptions, compatible parts and escalation procedures. Record who confirmed each item, when it was confirmed and the source. Next translation candidates are the rental and business handover guides, followed by selected reviewed articles. Phase VI needs separately confirmed coverage facts and authorized geographic changes.

It does not substantiate a service result. Other photos are held for privacy, protected details or insufficient context. Existing stock or provenance-unverified blog imagery is labelled as illustrative. There is no published completed-job claim based on the photos alone.

Use the attachment inventory and a redacted intake record with fields for service category, general area, problem, authorized work, verified outcome, photo provenance, permission, privacy review, and approved service references. Only verified entries with a safe, approved image and a meaningful customer takeaway can be considered for publication. The customer resource model provides the necessary headings, evidence links and canonical service relationships without requiring a dedicated empty portfolio.

Official source examples used in the content include manufacturer owner-manual resources from Ford and Toyota, Schlage on rekeying, NHTSA on a child alone in a locked car, OSHA on workplace exit routes and Allegion on the distinction between panic hardware and fire exit hardware. These are general or manufacturer-specific references. They do not verify the company’s capabilities for a particular vehicle or compliance of any opening. Public author identity and revision dates remain grounded in the original post and this documented editorial revision.
