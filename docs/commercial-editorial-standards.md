# Pro Locksmith Tech website editorial standards

- Make it easy to call Pro Locksmith Tech for routine vehicle, home and business locksmith problems. Explain what our technicians can do at the customer's location, including same-day and emergency availability. Explain the service in plain language and preserve useful distinctions between key cutting, programming, entry, rekeying, repair and replacement.
- Do not require callers to prepare documents, vehicle details, backup keys or parking arrangements. Dispatch coordinates the visit and technicians handle any required service authorization. Keep ordinary marketing pages focused on locksmith work and omit emergency-response scenarios and referrals to other providers. Operational and legal disclosures belong in the existing legal pages.
- Apply the same standards to English and Spanish copy, structured data, metadata and generated discovery content. Include only substantiated claims. Coverage-directory geography does not establish technician availability. Listings without confirmed coverage must offer a call to check.
- Preserve paid pages, noindex, tracking and conversion behavior. Record any paid copy findings separately until the owner approves an edit.
- Use authentic Google reviews. Preserve the reviewer's wording, mark excerpts and avoid inventing dates, ratings or service categories. Only use customer photographs when the owner approves website usage.
- Permanently exclude the playful Google lockout review identified by “licksmith” and “I want to get lockout again” and marked “Great price.” Its attachment depicts the owner and is also permanently excluded. Do not use the review, any excerpt, translation, screenshot, crop, derivative or its photo on any website or in generated website content. Keep the original review and attachment on Google untouched. This exclusion also applies to future imports, design revisions and review refreshes.
- Preserve the separate exclusion of the seven previously rejected images and their fourteen removed website URLs. The approved Our Work illustrations must be described as illustrations.
